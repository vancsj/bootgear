# Calibration oracle

These are targeted expectations, not claims that every other judgement is settled.
Use raw fixtures without this oracle for blind probes. Review unexpected findings against the actual request.

| Case | Required assessment or restraint | Evidence boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| expert-reference | Navigation Meets: ascending codes support expert lookup. Do not require a glossary, story arc or unspecified extra codes. | Memory alone cannot establish external Accuracy Meets. A local 500 precision judgement needs reader-impact evidence; it is not a mandatory defect outcome. |

## Comparison rules

Check required defects, required restraint and positive states separately.
Do not average them into a quality certificate. Preserve unexpected model judgements for source-based challenge.
A valid schema cannot prove that the model used the rubric correctly.
Record exact fixture and rubric hashes, provider, environment, commit and observed outputs.
